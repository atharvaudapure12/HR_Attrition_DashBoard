1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE & PROBLEM STATEMENT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The objective of this project is to develop an Executive HR Analytics Dashboard that enables Human Resource teams and business leaders to monitor workforce demographics, employee attrition, compensation trends, and employee satisfaction.

Employee attrition significantly impacts organizational productivity, recruitment costs, employee morale, and business continuity. The organization requires a centralized Business Intelligence solution to identify attrition patterns, understand workforce behavior, evaluate compensation structures, and support data-driven retention strategies.

The dashboard transforms employee records into actionable insights that assist HR professionals in improving workforce planning and reducing employee turnover.

The project answers critical business questions across three analytical areas:

Page 1: Executive Workforce Overview & Employee Demographics

Page 2: Employee Attrition Analysis & Retention Drivers

Page 3: Compensation, Performance & Employee Satisfaction Analysis


2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS (KPIs) & BUSINESS LOGIC
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

To evaluate workforce performance and employee retention, the dashboard implements the following HR KPIs using DAX.

* Total Employees

Formula =
Count of Employee Records

Business Purpose:
Measures the total workforce strength.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Attrition Count

Formula =
Count of Employees where Attrition = "Yes"

Business Purpose:
Measures the number of employees who left the organization.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Attrition Rate %

Formula =
(Attrition Count / Total Employees) × 100

Business Purpose:
Evaluates employee turnover.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Average Monthly Income

Formula =
Average(Monthly Income)

Business Purpose:
Measures average employee compensation.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Average Age

Formula =
Average(Employee Age)

Business Purpose:
Analyzes workforce age distribution.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Average Salary Hike %

Formula =
Average(Percent Salary Hike)

Business Purpose:
Measures annual employee salary increment.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Average Job Satisfaction

Formula =
Average(Job Satisfaction Score)

Business Purpose:
Evaluates employee engagement and workplace satisfaction.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Average Years at Company

Formula =
Average(Years At Company)

Business Purpose:
Measures average employee tenure and workforce stability.


3. DATA DICTIONARY (CRITICAL METRICS EXPLAINED)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

EmployeeNumber
Unique identifier assigned to each employee.

Age
Employee age in years.

Gender
Employee gender.

Department
Business department where the employee works.

JobRole
Employee designation within the organization.

EducationField
Academic background of the employee.

BusinessTravel
Frequency of employee business travel.

MonthlyIncome
Monthly salary earned by the employee.

PercentSalaryHike
Annual salary increment percentage.

JobSatisfaction
Employee satisfaction score.

EnvironmentSatisfaction
Employee satisfaction with workplace environment.

PerformanceRating
Employee performance evaluation score.

OverTime
Indicates whether the employee regularly works overtime.

YearsAtCompany
Total years employed within the organization.

YearsInCurrentRole
Years spent in the current job role.

YearsSinceLastPromotion
Time elapsed since last promotion.

TrainingTimesLastYear
Number of training sessions attended.

MaritalStatus
Employee marital status.

Attrition
Indicates whether the employee left the organization.

EmployeeCount
Total employee count available in the dataset.
